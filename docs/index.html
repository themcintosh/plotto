Plotto

A plot machine after William Wallace Cook, 1928

Press Generate to randomly configure one of 13,950 masterplots from William Wallace Cook’s PLOTTO. Behind it, the machine will build a fuller story from Cook’s blueprints. Open “Cook’s Blueprint” under the Masterplot to read it. Choose Interpret with AI and send the plot to an AI writer in the genre you pick.

AI writer

Interpret the plot

Choose a genre and press Interpret. The AI writer takes the masterplot and Cook’s Blueprint as raw material, shapes them into one story that makes sense, and writes it up as a treatment.

Plotto: A New Method of Plot Suggestion for Writers of Creative Fiction, by William Wallace Cook (1928). 1,462 conflicts; A is the male protagonist, B the female.
